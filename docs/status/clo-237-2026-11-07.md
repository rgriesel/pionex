The entry/range/exit/cost parameters were frozen in the function docstring
before the one evaluation, with a separately saved registration. Four fixed
configurations count as four trials, reserved before evaluation; append-only
outcome records have weight zero and do not recount them. Registry count 289
at evaluation. No winning parameter was selected, and nothing was rerun.

WHY: adverse directional inventory, costs and one liquidation overwhelm small
grid gains. The model does not copy the owner's discretionary entry or exit
timing. $10 minimum row notionals eventually strand remaining capital; 328
candidate entries are rejected, so most later days are inactive. Consequently
the daily-return Monte Carlo is diluted by inactivity and misses intraday
tails; the intrabar drawdown is the relevant conservative historical loss.
The 63% win rate is not an edge claim. Matched grid cycles earn $6.89 gross,
well below $77.88 total measured model costs; initial inventory exits have a
separate directional contribution and are not counted as matched grid cycles.

WHAT: lower exposure, wider row spacing and causal two-direction entries may
address these mechanisms, but must pass the same gates as a new test. A
$1.20 terminal advantage over initial inventory hold is not a near-miss:
both controls lose roughly 70%, far outside the target/drawdown criteria.
No candidate is retired solely from this approximation of the owner.
