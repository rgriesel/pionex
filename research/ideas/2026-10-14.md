## 1. Correlation-aware portfolio risk cap for the trend basket

- **Rule.** After the daily vote and the per-coin sizing (40%/N divided by 30-day vol), compute forecast basket
  volatility sigma_p = sqrt(w' S w) with S the same 30-day daily covariance of the 10 coins. If sigma_p is above
  a cap fixed before the test (the basket's own median forecast sigma_p in 2018-2020 only, then applied
  untouched from 2021), multiply all weights by cap / sigma_p. Votes, 2% band and the 100% gross limit stay as they are.
- **Holding period.** Unchanged (per-coin trend holds, median 3 d, mean 16 d). It only changes size.
- **Data.** Binance daily klines of the 10 coins. Have it.
- **Structural reason.** Current sizing gives each coin an equal risk share and ignores correlation. With 10
  coins IN, each contributing 4% of vol: basket vol is about 12.6% if they were uncorrelated, about 34% at
  correlation 0.7, and 40% at 1.0. Crypto correlations rise in sell-offs, so the basket is largest in risk
  exactly when diversification disappears. This is risk accounting, not alpha, and nobody has to be on the
  other side of it.
- **Evidence.** Baltas and Kosowski (SSRN 2140091) attribute weak time-series momentum after 2008 to rising
  pairwise correlations and report that a correlation-adjusted leverage rule helped and survived costs
  (futures, not crypto): https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2140091. Grobys et al. (Financial Markets and Portfolio
  Management, 2025) report that volatility management reduces crypto momentum crashes (weekly, top-30 coins,
  2016-2023): https://osuva.uwasa.fi/handle/11111/18877. Zarattini, Pagani and Barbon (SFI 25-80) support
  vol-sized long-only crypto trend but do not test a correlation cap: https://abarbon.com/papers/catching-crypto-trends
- **Falsified if.** Max drawdown does not fall by at least 3 points (26% to 23% or better) with at most a 10%
  relative loss of CAGR, in the 2021-2026 window and again in the 2018-2020 warm-up. Or it does not beat the
  control of lowering the 40% target uniformly to the same average exposure. Or the gain vanishes at 4x costs.
  Or the cap binds in fewer than 3 distinct episodes (then: inconclusive, not a pass).

## 2. DVOL shock cap (implied-vol warning light for the basket)

- **Rule.** On any day when Deribit BTC DVOL at 00:00 UTC is at least 1.25x its trailing 90-day median, halve all
  basket target weights; return to full size when it falls below that level. One parameter set, fixed in
  advance; 1.5x is the only allowed sensitivity run.
- **Holding period.** Episodes of about 1 to 4 weeks; the underlying coin holds are unchanged.
- **Data.** Deribit public `get_volatility_index_data`, free. The store has 2023-01 on; it needs a backfill to the
  index start (about March 2021, verify at fetch) so the May 2022 and Nov 2022 crashes are in the sample.
- **Structural reason.** Options market makers and hedgers price forward event risk. The basket's only vol
  input is trailing 30-day realized vol, which lags a regime shift by days. Implied vol moves first, and the
  people paying for hedges are the informed warning. The weakness: vol timing only pays if expected returns do not
  rise with vol.
- **Evidence.** Moreira and Muir (NBER 22208; equities and currency carry): https://www.nber.org/papers/w22208.
  Critique showing real-time failure for many strategies: Cederburg et al., JFE 2020,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.04.015. Bitcoin VIX construction and its variance premium: Alexander and
  Imeraj, https://sro.sussex.ac.uk/id/eprint/91094/. Crypto-specific vol management of momentum: Grobys et al. (link in card 1).
- **Falsified if.** The lab's own screen leans the other way: DVOL level and 24h change had positive IC with BTC
  forward 72h returns (about +0.07 to +0.08, t about 1.6 to 1.7, not significant; `var/logs/factors_screen_partial.log`).
  If that is real, cutting after spikes gives up rebounds. So the card fails if: the basket's mean next-7d and
  next-30d return after trigger days is not below untriggered days; or max-drawdown cut is under 3 points; or
  Sharpe is below a control that halves on the same number of random days (block bootstrap); or all of the
  benefit comes from the May-Nov 2022 stretch. Only about 5.5 years of DVOL exist, so expect few episodes.

## 3. Variance-premium sleeve: deploy idle cash into BTC and ETH when the premium is high

- **Rule.** At 00:00 UTC, when BTC VRP (DVOL/100 minus 30-day realized vol, as defined in `swing/factors.py`) is in
  the top third of its own trailing 365-day distribution AND the basket's BTC and ETH votes are both OUT, buy BTC
  and ETH with idle cash (at most 20% of NAV in total). Sell after 14 days regardless; no re-entry for 7 days.
- **Holding period.** 14 days, fixed.
- **Data.** DVOL (see card 2) plus BTC and ETH daily klines. Have it, with the same backfill need.
- **Structural reason.** Implied minus realized variance is the price of insurance and of upside demand. In
  equities a high premium has preceded higher returns because it tracks risk aversion; spot holders earn the
  compensation. In crypto the premium may instead reflect speculative call demand: Almeida et al. find it highest in
  the low-volatility regime. So the mechanism is unsettled and the test must stay agnostic. The sleeve uses cash
  the trend system leaves idle, and is deliberately counter-trend to the basket.
- **Evidence.** Bollerslev, Tauchen and Zhou, RFS 2009 (S&P 500, strongest at quarterly horizon):
  https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=948309. Almeida, Grith, Miftachov and Wang, arXiv 2410.15195 (Bitcoin premia by
  volatility regime): https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15195. Lab hint, not significant: BTC `vrp` IC about +0.06 to +0.07 at
  24h/72h, but negative in 2026 at 72h (-0.07).
- **Falsified if.** Mean 14-day net return after 0.12% round-trip costs is at or below zero, or its bootstrap lower
  bound is below zero by the lab's usual rule; fewer than about 25 non-overlapping entries (then: inconclusive,
  track forward); the sign differs between 2021-22 and 2023-26; one quarter drives the result; or it does not beat
  entering on random cash days at the same frequency. This is also the "catching a falling knife" risk in a long bear market.
  Expect low power.

## 4. Stablecoin-supply contraction gate

- **Rule.** If total USD-stablecoin supply (DefiLlama) is at or below its level 30 days earlier, halve all basket
  target weights; otherwise unchanged. A daily value is used only from the next UTC day (existing lab rule).
- **Holding period.** Weeks to months per episode.
- **Data.** DefiLlama `stablecoincharts/all`, free; stored since 2017-11 in `alt.db`. Have it.
- **Structural reason.** Stablecoins are the cash that buys crypto. Net mint means fiat entering; net
  redemption means fiat leaving. Mint and redeem go through a few KYC counterparties, so the flow is slow and
  trending rather than noisy. Biggest weakness: causality can run backwards, because issuance follows price
  declines, so the gate may only restate the trend vote.
- **Evidence.** Ante, Fiedler and Strehle, event study of 565 issuances (issuance follows downturns; positive
  abnormal returns around it): https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/2020/06/24/how-do-stablecoin-issuances-affect-cryptocurrency-markets/.
  Griffin and Shams, JF 2020 (Tether issuance, 2017-18 episode; contested): https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=3195066.
  Chi, Chu and Hao (USDT net inflows to exchanges predict BTC and ETH at 1-6 h, 2017-2023; needs on-chain data we lack):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06327. Jones, Matsui and Knottenbelt, 2026 (in-sample causality daily to monthly;
  stablecoin-aware vol targeting reduced risk): https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23480.
- **Falsified if.** Lab screen: `stables_7d` and `stables_30d` had IC about 0 at 4h with the wrong sign in 2022
  (24h/72h not in the partial log). The card fails if, on days the basket is mostly IN, forward 30-day basket return
  is not lower in contraction months than in growth months; if it adds nothing once the basket's own trend vote
  is controlled for; or if contraction months come from a single episode (likely 2022-23). Fewer than 3 episodes
  means "cannot be tested, track forward", not a pass.

## 5. US equity-stress gate (VIX) for the trend basket

- **Rule.** When the VIX close is at least 30, halve all basket target weights; restore full size when it closes
  below 25. Fixed in advance, no grid. The value of day D is used from D+1 00:00 UTC.
- **Holding period.** Episodes of days to weeks.
- **Data.** FRED `VIXCLS` (free, daily, 1990 on); the fetcher is in `altdata.py` but nothing is stored. For the live
  paper book FRED posts the next morning, so use a same-evening source or lag one more day.
- **Structural reason.** Since 2020 crypto's marginal buyers are levered, cross-asset risk takers, so margin calls
  and de-risking in equities spill into crypto. The basket's 20/60/120-day votes react slowly to a one-day
  cross-asset shock. The problem: the gate fires after the move and may sell the low.
- **Evidence.** IMF blog (Adrian, Iyer, Qureshi, Jan 2022): Bitcoin-S&P 500 return correlation rose from about
  0.01 in 2017-19 to about 0.36 in 2020-21: https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2022/01/11/crypto-prices-move-more-in-sync-with-stocks-posing-new-risks.
  Against it: Liu and Tsyvinski (RFS 2021) found early crypto returns essentially unrelated to stock and macro
  factors: https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877.
- **Falsified if.** After trigger days the next-7d and next-30d basket returns are not below normal days; max-drawdown
  cut under 3 points; it loses to the same-exposure random-days control; or all of the effect comes from 2020-03 and
  2022. About 8 to 10 VIX episodes exist since 2018, so one cluster can decide it. Lowest prior of the five; DVOL
  (card 2) may already capture it.
